01. About

From IT Support to Cybersecurity

A hands-on journey of continuous learning and practical security.

I started my career in IT support and systems administration, spending 11 years troubleshooting hardware, software and network issues across Windows and mixed environments. That foundation gave me a deep understanding of how systems break — and how to fix them.

Over the last 13 years, I led teams of 50+ people in high-pressure hospitality operations, sharpening my incident management, prioritisation and communication skills. Today, I'm channelling that experience into cybersecurity, with a focus on SOC operations, threat detection and log analysis.

I learn by building: Wazuh SIEM labs, Kali Linux attack simulations, network traffic analysis with Wireshark, and wireless security projects with Flipper Zero and Pwnagotchi.
